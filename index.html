The Pixel Gaunt Mission

Pixel Gaunt is a premier modern retro games portal dedicated to indie game developers. Founded in 2026, our platform bridges the nostalgic gap between classic arcade thrills and powerful modern web technologies.

We provide a rich ecosystem where players can enjoy blazing fast HTML5 games, and creators can upload their titles, earn up to 80% ad revenue share, and connect with a lively multiplayer tournament community. Empowering creativity is embedded in our DNA.

Get in Touch

Whether you're a developer looking to host your game, a player reporting a bug, or just want to say hi, our team is always ready to assist.

  • Email: pixelgaunt@gmail.com
  • Community: Join our Discord via the footer icon.
  • Response Time: Typically within 24-48 business hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I upload my game?
A: Register an account, navigate to the Creator Profile Dashboard, and upload your compiled HTML5 zip folder.

Q: Is there a file size limit?
A: Yes, to maintain ultra-fast load times, currently all game assets must be compressed under 25MB.

Q: How does tournament revenue work?
A: Tournaments are funded via micro-transactions. We charge a tiny platform maintenance fee, and the remainder forms the massive prize pool for the victor!

Privacy & Data Policy

Your privacy is of utmost importance to Pixel Gaunt. We only collect the necessary data required to maintain your user account and leaderboard rankings.

We utilise Google Firebase for secure authentication. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Minimal browser cookies are used only to save your site preferences (like theme colours and sound toggles).

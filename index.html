Pixel Gaunt (pixelgaunt.com) is a modern retro games portal and digital studio founded by Pixel Gaunt. We develop and publish high-quality HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript browser-based games.


Our games are designed for seamless cross-platform play, supporting PC, Android, iOS, Mac, and Linux. We focus on delivering polished gaming experiences with beautiful graphics, neon effects, and smooth gameplay.

We'd love to hear from you! Whether it's feedback, bug reporting for our games (like Stickman Velocity or Panda the Ocean Hunter), or business inquiries.

Community: Join our WhatsApp Group for direct bug reporting and updates.
